# 0.0.14.27 — original hair and texture placement repaired

The bald-looking player was caused by a shared texture import defect. The original loader uploads 2048-byte sectors as 32 VRAM words by 32 rows, with horizontal tiles first. The importer treated their bytes as a whole-image row-major stream. This scrambled the face and armor and made 52 of 86 sampled main-head UV centroids select transparent index 0. The decoder now reconstructs the intended image before indexed texture and material-cache cooking. The original head mesh, hair polygons, UVs, palettes, skeleton and poses are preserved. No hair was modeled by hand.

The newly compiled native port now displays original spiky hair and coherent face/armor in genuine Azahar captures. This focused repair is complete; **World Comes Alive remains unfinished** and PHYSICS_1TO1=false.
